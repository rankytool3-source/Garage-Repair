Our 21-Point Garage Door Inspection & Service

1

High-Grade Lubrication

Application of specialized synthetic lubricant to springs, hinges, rollers, bearing plates, and opener tracks to eliminate noise and friction wear.

2

Spring Tension Calibration

Checking torsion and extension spring balance to ensure the door opens manually with under 10 lbs of physical force.

3

Cable Fray Inspection

Detailed visual and physical inspection of galvanized steel lift cables for rust, broken strands, or drum slippage.

4

Roller Wear Assessment

Examining steel or nylon rollers for flat spots, wobbling stems, or worn ball bearings that cause track binding.

5

Track Alignment & Fasteners

Tightening all wall jamb brackets, track bolts, and hinges while verifying vertical and horizontal track plumb.

6

Safety Sensor Testing

Testing photo-eye auto-reverse function with physical obstruction blocks and recalibrating infrared beam optics.

Why Annual Garage Door Maintenance is Essential

Your garage door is typically the largest moving mechanical structure in your house, performing over 1,500 open/close cycles per year. Over time, vibration causes bolts to loosen, factory grease to dry out, and spring tension to shift.

Routine maintenance catches minor component wear before it turns into a catastrophic spring snap, burnt opener motor, or off-track failure in Newnan, GA.
