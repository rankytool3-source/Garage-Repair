Garage Door Problems We Solve in Newnan, GA

Our technicians arrive fully prepared with commercial-tier replacement components to handle all common and complex overhead door failures.

Broken Garage Door Springs

We replace snapped torsion and extension springs with high-cycle oil-tempered springs rated for 20,000+ operations. Never attempt spring DIY due to dangerous high-tension forces.

Garage Door Opener Problems

From motor gear replacement and logic board failures to chain or quiet belt drive repair, we fix all major brands including LiftMaster, Genie, and Craftsman.

Broken Cables & Wire Drums

Frayed or snapped cables cause the door to sag or jam. We replace worn aircraft-grade steel cables and reset cable drums for smooth lift balance.

Damaged Panels & Sections

Dented or cracked garage door panels compromise security and curb appeal. We replace individual damaged door sections matching your exact model and color.

Garage Door Tracks & Alignment

Bent or misaligned tracks prevent smooth travel and can cause the door to jump out of track. We realign, straighten, or replace damaged vertical/horizontal tracks.

Rollers, Hinges & Hardware

Worn steel rollers create grinding noise and resistance. We install heavy-duty nylon ball-bearing rollers and reinforced steel hinges for whisper-quiet operation.

Safety Sensor Alignment

If your door starts closing and immediately reverses, misaligned photo-eye safety sensors are often to blame. We realign, clean, or wire new safety sensors.

Remote & Keypad Reprogramming

Unresponsive clickers, keyless entry keypads, and smart device connectivity issues are diagnosed and reprogrammed with secure rolling-code technology.

Hinges & Structural Reinforcement

We replace cracked hinges and install horizontal steel reinforcement struts to strengthen sagging panels and prevent structural door collapse.
